# In-shop sales demo: the script

For the founder and for field agents in India. Written 2026-11-20 for the `/marketing` demo (plan: in-shop sales demo). Revyu is for local shops of every kind: kirana, salon, cafe, bakery, mobile shop, gym, clinic, and so on.

Voice: plain, straight, on their side. Say "every customer", never "happy customers". The Hinglish lines are meant to be said as written; the English under each one is what it means. The rules in section 6 come before any sale.

---

## 1. The visit at a glance (3 minutes)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | When the shop is quiet: usually 11:00-12:00 or 15:00-17:00. Never walk in during a rush or while the owner is serving someone. If they are busy, say "Main 20 minute mein aata hoon" ("I'll come back in 20 minutes") and do. |
| **Who** | The owner, or whoever decides on money. Ask the staff: "Malik ji hain? 2 minute chahiye." ("Is the owner in? I need 2 minutes.") Do the demo with the staff only if the owner says to. |
| **Have ready** | Phone charged (above 50%), mobile data on, screen brightness up. `/marketing` open and **unlocked with your PIN**. The shop **already searched and its demo built** before you walk in (search it outside, check it is the right listing and address). |
| **Never** | Leave the phone unlocked on the counter, tell anyone the PIN, or let a customer use your phone. |

The demo has 4 screens: **Google today**, **Try it**, **Your dashboard**, **Start free**. The "Say this" strip at the top of each screen has short cues; this page is the full version.

## 2. The opener

> "Namaste, main Revyu se hoon. Aapki shop ka Google pe ek cheez dikhani thi, 2 minute. Aapke {M} reviews hain, paas mein {Rival} ke {N}. Maine aapke liye kuch banaya hai, dikhaun?"
>
> *Hello, I'm from Revyu. I want to show you one thing about your shop on Google, 2 minutes. You have {M} reviews; {Rival} nearby has {N}. I've built something for you, can I show you?*

Read {M}, {N} and {Rival} off the screen. Never round them, never guess them. If the shop already leads, say that instead: "Aap paas mein sabse aage ho. Aage rehne ke liye har hafte naye reviews chahiye." ("You lead nearby. Staying ahead needs new reviews every week.") If they say no, thank them and leave. No second push.

## 3. The four screens

### Screen 1: "{Shop} on Google today"
- **On screen:** their real rating and review count ("Live from Google"), the top nearby rival, and a ranked list of them plus 3 nearby shops.
- **Say:** "Customer jab Google pe search karta hai, dono shops saath dikhti hain. Jitne zyada customers se puchoge, utne zyada log likhenge."
  *When a customer searches Google, both shops show up together. The more customers you ask, the more people write.*
- **Let them:** look at the numbers. Pause. Let them say the rival's name first.
- **Don't say:** "You'll pass {Rival} in 2 months", "You'll be number 1", or anything bad about the rival. Don't say the rival breaks any rule.

### Screen 2: "This goes on your counter"
- **On screen:** a standee with their shop name and a real QR.
- **Say:** "Ye standee aapke counter pe, aapke naam ke saath. Apne phone se scan kijiye, bilkul waise jaise customer karega."
  *This stands on your counter with your name on it. Scan it with your own phone, exactly as a customer would.*
- **Let them:** scan it **on their own phone** and go through it: stars, tags, the draft, then the Google step (a labelled stand-in page, not their real listing). Let them try a low star too, so they see the Google button is still there.
- **After they try:** "Type kuch nahi, bas tap. Draft unke apne taps se. Google ka button har customer ko, naraaz ko bhi. Yahi Google ka rule hai, isliye profile safe."
  *Nothing to type, just taps. The draft comes only from their own taps. Every customer gets the Google button, even an unhappy one. That is Google's rule, which is why your profile stays safe.*
- **Don't say:** "Only the 5-star ones go to Google", "Let's post one now to test it". Don't take their phone from them. The demo link stops working after 3 hours and records nothing.

### Screen 3: "Your dashboard"
- **On screen:** their dashboard. Cards marked **"Live from Google"** are real; cards marked **"Example"** are example activity.
- **Say:** "Upar aapki asli Google rating, har hafte check. Ye paas ki shops ke saath race, har Monday. Koi naraaz hua toh private message aapko, yahin se call ya WhatsApp. Ye activity numbers example hain; asli pehle scan se."
  *At the top is your real Google rating, checked every week. This is you against nearby shops, every Monday. If someone is unhappy, the private message comes to you; call or WhatsApp them back from here. These activity numbers are an example; the real ones start from the first scan.*
- **Let them:** scroll it themselves.
- **Don't say:** that the example numbers are what they will get. Always say "example" out loud.

### Screen 4: "Start free today"
- **On screen:** 15 days free, the monthly price, about the per-day cost, what is included, the trust line, and a sign-up QR.
- **Say:** "15 din free, aaj kuch charge nahi; trial mein cancel kiya toh ek rupaya nahi. Phir {price} mahina, din ka lagbhag {perDay}. Abhi aapke saath set kar deta hoon, ye QR scan kijiye, shop pehle se bhari hai."
  *15 days free, nothing charged today; cancel in the trial and you pay nothing. Then {price} a month, about {perDay} a day. I'll set it up with you now: scan this QR, your shop is already filled in.*
- Read {price} and {perDay} **off the screen**. Never quote a price from memory, and never offer your own discount.

## 4. The close and signing up on the spot

1. Ask once: "Aaj shuru karein, ya kis din aake set kar doon?" ("Shall we start today, or which day should I come and set it up?")
2. If yes: they **scan the sign-up QR on their own phone** (Monthly or Yearly, their choice). Shop, category and plan are already filled in. They enter their own name, phone and email. If their phone won't scan, use "Fill it in on this phone" and hand them your phone **only for that form**, then take it back.
3. Tell them what happens next: "Hum aaj aapki shop verify karenge, phir QR live." ("We verify your shop today, then your QR goes live.") The trial starts from verification, not from today.
4. **The founder approves the sign-up at `/admin/approvals` the same day**, so the 15-day trial and the first charge fall on the dates the owner was told. Agents: message the founder the shop name as soon as they sign up.
5. Place the QR (SOP C): at the payment counter, at eye level, facing the customer. Teach the staff one line, said to **every** customer: "Would you scan this and share how your visit went? It takes a minute."
6. Tap **End demo** before you leave the shop. It clears the shop from your phone.

## 5. Objection cards

| They say | You say (honest, short) |
| --- | --- |
| "Google ka QR toh free hai." *(Google's QR is free.)* | "Free QR gives a blank box. We draft the first line from their taps, bring complaints to you privately, and show weekly numbers against nearby shops." |
| "Sirf khush customers ko Google bhejo." *(Send only happy customers to Google.)* | "Nahi. That's review gating; Google removes reviews and acts on the profile for it, your profile, not ours. Every customer sees Google; an unhappy one can also tell you privately first." |
| "Guarantee doge?" *(Will you guarantee results?)* | "No guarantees. You'll see scans and your Google count every week, and you get 15 free days to judge." |
| "Staff bhool jayega." *(Staff will forget.)* | "The QR asks every customer by itself. I'll teach your staff one line, and the dashboard shows you when scans drop." |
| "Mehenga hai." *(It's expensive.)* | "About {perDay} a day. One extra customer a month covers it, and the first 15 days are free." |
| "Google penalty toh nahi lagega?" *(Won't Google penalise me?)* | "It's built to Google's rules: the Google button for everyone, no rewards, and the customer writes and posts it themselves." |
| "Draft fake lagega." *(The draft will look fake.)* | "It's built only from the customer's own taps. They edit it and post it from their own account." |
| "Cancel kaise karenge?" *(How do I cancel?)* | "Any time from your dashboard. Cancel in the trial and you pay nothing." |
| "Hum already bolte hain." *(We already ask.)* | "Staff ask the people they chat with, when they remember. The QR asks everyone, every time." |
| "Details bhejo / sochenge." *(Send details / we'll think.)* | "Start the free trial now, nothing is charged. Or tell me a day and I'll set it up in 10 minutes." |

## 6. Compliance: do and don't (field agents)

Breaking any of these ends the visit and can cost the owner their Google profile. When unsure, say less.

**Do**
- Say "every customer, every time". The QR and the WhatsApp line go to everyone.
- Say "the customer writes it and posts it from their own account".
- Show the Google button at a low star when they try the demo.
- Read every number off the screen, and say "example" for example cards.

**Don't**
- **Never post a review on the owner's listing, or ask anyone to, even as a test.** Not you, not the owner, not the staff, not a friend.
- Never promise a number of reviews, a rating, or a rank ("you'll get 50 reviews", "you'll reach 4.8", "you'll be top on Maps").
- Never say "only happy customers" or "only 5-star" go to Google.
- Never offer gifts, discounts or freebies to customers for reviews, and never suggest the owner does.
- Never hand your phone to a customer to write a review.
- Never say a named rival breaks Google's rules, buys reviews or uses any tool.
- Never invent results ("a shop near here got 100 reviews").
- Never use a fake deadline ("price goes up tomorrow", "only today").

## 7. After the visit

1. **Log it in the visit sheet** before the next shop: shop, area, date, who you met, outcome (signed up / set-up day booked / not now / no), next step.
2. No personalised link is sent after the visit. This is by design: the demo link expires in 3 hours and nothing about the shop is saved.
3. If they didn't sign up, follow up **once** by WhatsApp the next day, plain:
   > "Namaste {Name} ji, kal Revyu ke liye milne aaya tha. 15 din free hai, kuch charge nahi. Kis din aake set kar doon? 10 minute lagenge."
   > *Hello {Name}, I visited yesterday about Revyu. It's 15 days free, nothing charged. Which day should I come and set it up? It takes 10 minutes.*
4. If they pick a day, book it in the sheet and confirm the evening before. If they don't reply, leave it; no chasing.
